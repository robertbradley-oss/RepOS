01Workflow

One ticket, start to finish,
without leaving the screen.

Most support tools make reps juggle tabs for orders, receipts, macros, and policy docs. RepOS keeps all of it in the ticket.

  1. Triage

    Scan the whole queue in one pass.

    Open, Assigned To Me, and Closed tabs sit over a dense, sortable table. Priority, SLA risk, and owner are visible at a glance, and duplicates merge right from the toolbar.

  2. Context

    Know the customer before you type.

    Order, model, purchase source, receipt, and warranty status sit next to the thread. Past tickets and verified receipts are one click away, not one search away.

  3. Reply

    Answer with the team's best reply.

    Macros drop in with the customer's name, order, and model already filled. Internal notes stay amber, and the composer always shows who will see what you write.

  4. Manage

    See where the queue is heading.

    Managers get SLA triage, real trend lines, and workload by rep, with drill-throughs into the exact tickets behind every number.

02RepOS Assist

Drafts, not decisions.

Assist reads the thread and your workspace's policies, then hands the rep a starting point. It never sends anything on its own.

  • Reps stay in charge. Every suggestion lands as an editable draft, clearly labeled until a person sends it.
  • Grounded in your workspace. Policies, macros, and product notes come from your Knowledge Vault, not the open internet.
  • Admins decide who gets it. Assist is switched on per workspace and per role.

03Teams

Built for the whole support floor.

Everyone works in the same workspace. Each role sees what it needs and nothing it shouldn't.

A queue for scanning and a ticket screen for working one case deeply. Tickets open on the latest message, with everything else in the side rail.

Leads

04Details

The small things reps notice.

  • 01
    Opens where you left off.Tickets jump to the latest customer message, not the top of a long thread.
  • 02
    Macros that fill themselves in.Customer name, order, and model are added to the draft for you.
  • 03
    Merge from the queue.Select duplicates in the table and combine them without opening either one.
  • 04
    Receipts inline.PDF and screenshot receipts open in a preview with order, source, and dates attached.
  • 05
    Search the whole queue.Filters apply across every page before pagination, not just the 50 rows in view.
  • 06
    Fair-share assignment.New tickets spread across the active pool, so no one gets buried.
  • 07
    One-click ticket links.Copy a link to any ticket, or to a whole selection, straight from the toolbar.
  • 08
    Always clear who's reading.Reply and Internal note are separate tabs, and the composer labels what's customer visible.
